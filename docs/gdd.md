## 1. Executive summary

Carrot Clash is a 3v3 / 4v4 team FPS set in a stylized food-themed world. Players choose from four vegetable-inspired hero classes and fight to control objectives on a three-zone map. The defining mechanic is a **momentum/combo system**: kills escalate your power across three tiers, but death transfers that charge to your killer. Every engagement becomes a risk-reward calculation.

Matches run 8 minutes. Short session length plus high TTK variance (from momentum) creates the same "one more game" pull as Apex and The Finals.

## 2. Design pillars

| # | Pillar | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Momentum over camping** | Decay punishes passivity; buffs reward kills. The game physically pushes you forward |
| 2 | **Class synergy** | 4 classes with complementary kits; team composition matters but isn't mandatory |
| 3 | **Readable chaos** | Bright colour language per class; abilities are clearly telegraphed; chaos is fun, not frustrating |
| 4 | **Theme-first identity** | The food theme drives ability names, audio design, map geography, and SFX. It's not cosmetic |

## 4. Gameplay synopsis

<span class="cc-status built">Implemented</span> The match flow (state machine, timer, scoring, Zone C unlock, sudden death, win resolution) lives in `GameModeManager`. Scenes and the player prefab that host it are editor-pending.

**Pre-match:** Players select a class in a 30-second lobby. No class lock; duplicates allowed.

**Match start:** Teams spawn 60m apart. Zones A and B are immediately contested. Zone C (Central Stage) is locked behind a barrier until minute 5.

**Mid-match:** Teams earn points from objective time plus kills. Kill momentum escalates per-player power. Tier 3 "On Fire" players are visually distinct targets; killing them transfers their momentum charge to you.

**Late-game:** Zone C unlocks at minute 5, scoring at 2x rate. Most teams pivot entirely to contesting it. If neither team holds Zone C, Zones A and B decide the match. Score caps at 500 for an early win; otherwise highest score at 8:00 wins. If the score is tied at 8:00 the match enters a 60-second **Sudden Death** overtime (next score wins) before resolving to a draw.

**Post-match:** MVP card (top score contribution), Hot Streak award (longest momentum chain), XP earned, rank delta. Kills, highlights, and XP rewards play out with delay to keep the player watching the screen and maximize dopamine drip.

::: info Code delta
`MatchStats` resolves the **MVP** by score contribution and the **Hot Streak** by `LongestOnFireStreak`, matching the design above. The full kill-chain *replay clip* (§5.3) is not implemented; only the streak number is tracked, and the shareable clip is post-MVP. The post-match XP breakdown values come from `XPManager` / `BattlePassService`.
:::

## 5. Player psychology / retention hooks

These are load-bearing engagement mechanics, not cosmetic additions:

### 5.1 Variable reward loops
- Kill streaks are variable; you don't know when tier 3 hits, which is why they feel amazing
- Post-match XP breakdown shows rewards with animated counters, so even a loss gives visible progress
- Daily challenges randomize which class/objective they target, so there's always something fresh to chase

### 5.2 Near-miss engineering
- Score cap at 500; most matches end 420-480, so every match feels like it could have gone either way
- Tiebreaker zone (Zone C) almost always determines the winner, which creates a clear dramatic moment
- Momentum transfer means your death directly empowered the enemy. The stake is personal, not random

### 5.3 Social hooks
- Post-match screen shows your team's combined momentum uptime, a shared accomplishment
- "Hot Streak" award shows kill-chain replay clip (short 10s highlight, shareable) <span class="cc-status pending">Editor-pending</span>. The streak metric is tracked in code; the replay clip and share are post-MVP
- Party system: invite friends directly from post-match screen <span class="cc-status pending">Editor-pending</span>. Depends on Lobby, which ships with NGO (not installed)

### 5.4 Session length design
- 8-minute matches mean a minimal time commitment, no friction to "just one more"
- Instant respawn means no dead time and no sitting out; every second is gameplay
- Class select is 30 seconds max: not long enough to overthink, fast enough to feel reactive

## 6. Art direction

**Style:** Stylized and slightly exaggerated, with saturated colour. Think Overwatch's readability plus Fortnite's colour saturation, but grounded in a food/market aesthetic rather than cartoony.

**Colour language (class identification):** <span class="cc-status built">Implemented</span> (set on each `CharacterDataSO` via the data generator; art/VFX that read these are editor-pending)
- Carrot: Orange primary, leafy-green accents
- Jalapeño: Hot-red primary, ember-orange accents
- Broccoli: Deep-green primary, light-floret-green accents
- Potato: Earthy-brown primary, tan accents

::: info Code delta
The shipped `primaryColor` / `accentColor` values differ slightly from the original accent intent: Jalapeño's accent is **ember orange** (not deep green), Broccoli's accent is **light floret green** (not white), and Potato's accent is **tan** (not grey). Primaries are unchanged. These are the colours the HUD/kill-feed/momentum-ring tint logic reads today. Adjust the SOs if the art direction wants the original accents back.
:::

**Map palette:** Warm yellows and oranges in the Courtyard, cool blues and greens inside the Market, neutral warm whites on the Central Stage. Colour shifts help spatial awareness.

**Momentum visual language:** <span class="cc-status partial">Partial</span>. Tier state, the screen-edge `OnFireOverlay`, the momentum ring tint and tier-up audio cues are implemented. The per-tier character VFX (outline, trail, full FX) are editor-pending prefabs that hook the `MomentumTier` colour from `MomentumConfigSO.TierColor`.
- Tier 0 (Cold): No effect; grey ring
- Tier 1 (Warm): Faint colour-matching outline glow on player model
- Tier 2 (Hot): Particle trail (leaves, embers; class-appropriate)
- Tier 3 (On Fire): Full character VFX, screen edge glow in player POV, audible heartbeat SFX

## 7. Audio direction

<span class="cc-status partial">Partial</span> The audio engine is implemented: a pooled `AudioManager` (32-source pool, 25m max distance), `AudioLibrary` key resolver, surface-aware `FootstepController` + `FootstepBank`, a layered `MusicDirector` and `AmbientZone` triggers. All of it is wired to the SFX-key conventions in `CONTRACTS.md`. What's pending is the actual content: every clip is editor-authored and not yet imported.

- **Music:** Energetic, kitchen/market-themed base track. Intensity layers up with match tension (under 2 minutes, Zone C contested). Momentum tier 3 triggers a personal audio "rush" layer. (`MusicDirector` plays base/intensity/tier3 layers via `PlayMusic` + `SetMusicParameter`.)
- **Footsteps:** Surface-responsive (wood market floor, stone courtyard, metal grating on catwalks; surfaces ∈ {stone, wood, metal, dirt}). Carrot's Silent Steps passive reduces these to near-zero when moving below 50% sprint speed.
- **Abilities:** Each ability has a food-themed SFX motif (Spice Burst = sizzle + burst, Leaf Shield = rustling + thud, Earthen Slam = bass thud + crumble).
- **UI:** Minimal, punchy. Momentum tier-up is a satisfying "power-up" chime. Kill confirmed is a short distinct crack. Zone captured is a clear resonant tone.

## 9. Monetization (post-MVP)

<span class="cc-status partial">Partial</span> The underlying progression spine is implemented: XP, a 50-tier battle pass, daily/seasonal challenges and a save system (`XPManager`, `BattlePassService`, `ChallengeSystem`, `SaveSystem`, `ProgressionService`). No store, currency or purchasing exists; cosmetics are content and out of vertical-slice scope.

Not in vertical slice scope. Design now to avoid painting into a corner.

- **Cosmetics only**: no pay-to-win, no stat-affecting purchases
- **Battle Pass** (seasonal, 50 tiers): character skins, weapon skins, ability VFX recolors, sprays, victory poses
- **Direct store**: individual cosmetic items; no loot boxes (avoids gambling regulation risk)
- **Free-to-play**: base game free; battle pass is the primary revenue driver
- All four base classes free; any future classes also free (Fortnite/Apex model)

::: info Code delta: input bindings

## 11. Accessibility

Carrot Clash targets a broad audience. Accessibility features expand the player base and cost little to implement early, so they're in from the start rather than bolted on.

<span class="cc-status partial">Partial</span> `SettingsService` (PlayerPrefs-backed) already stores every toggle below: colourblind mode, reduce motion, high-contrast HUD, text scale, subtitles, aim assist, and full sensitivity/FOV/quality options. `ScreenEffects`/`PlayerCamera` honour the reduce-motion shake toggle today. The remaining work is editor-pending: the colourblind palettes, high-contrast HUD variants and subtitle captions are content/UI bindings, not new code.

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Colourblind modes | Protanopia / Deuteranopia / Tritanopia presets; alters class colour identifiers and momentum ring colours |
| Reduce motion | Disables screen shake, camera bob, heavy VFX animations (momentum tier effects simplified) |
| High contrast HUD | Increases contrast on HP bar, momentum ring, zone indicators |
| Text size | Normal / Large text globally |
| Subtitles | In-game voice callout text (e.g. "Enemy spotted" caption); Phase 5 scope |
| Controller aim assist | Rotational aim assist for gamepad input (toggle) |
| Remappable controls | All bindings remappable on PC; controller layout presets for console-style mapping |

All options in Settings → Accessibility.

## 12. Mobile-specific design

URP mobile profile is already configured. Additional mobile considerations:

| Concern | Solution |
|---|---|
| Touch controls | Virtual joystick (left), fire/ADS/ability buttons (right); see [UI/UX](ui-ux.md) mobile layout |
| Match length | 8-minute matches suit mobile sessions; no modification needed |
| Battery / thermal | Adaptive Performance asset already in project; use for dynamic quality scaling |
| Smaller screen | HUD scales proportionally; critical elements (HP, momentum ring) remain central |
| Network tolerance | NGO lag compensation max 200ms handles typical mobile network variance |
| Frame target | 30 FPS stable preferred over 60 FPS unstable on mobile |
